About Agra

Agra is a famous city in Uttar Pradesh. It is known for the beautiful Taj Mahal. Agra has a rich history, culture and architecture.

Famous Places

Agra has many famous places such as Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Mehtab Bagh.

Famous Food

Agra is famous for delicious food like Petha, Bedai and Mughlai food.
