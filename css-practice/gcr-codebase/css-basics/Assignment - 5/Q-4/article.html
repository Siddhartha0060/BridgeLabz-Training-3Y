The Role of Technology in Education

Author: Siddharth Singh

Publication Date: 25 August 2026

Technology has become an important part of modern education. Students can use computers and the internet to learn new topics. Online learning has made education easier and more accessible.

Teachers can use technology to make their classes more interesting. Videos, presentations and educational websites help students understand difficult topics in a simple way.

The use of digital tools also allows students to study at their own speed. They can watch recorded lectures and practice different questions whenever they want.

"Technology is a useful tool when it helps students learn better and makes education more accessible."
